# Surface brief: daniakash.com site (homepage leads)

Scope: all routes of daniakash.com; homepage is the lead surface. Mode: Persuade on home; Read on posts and newsletter issues; Experience-lite on projects, speaking, uses.

Audience: developers and AI builders. Action: explore the work (BrowserOS, projects, talks), then read and follow. Proof: real projects, 29 talks, 16 posts, GitHub numbers from projects.json, real photos. Constraints: every URL, feed and structured-data entity preserved; content unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dani's whole world on one tidy bento grid, visible at a glance; refuses both the dark terminal dev portfolio and the text-only minimal index.

OWN-WORLD: warm off-white ground, flat white tiles with large rounded corners, no shadows or gradients; tiles colour-coded by content type (blue active projects and now-building, yellow numbers, pink speaking, green open source and community (newsletter), ink feature tiles; dark-theme ink stays a near-neutral, never a hue); Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist body, Geist Mono for dates and data only; full-bleed real photos as tiles. Dark theme: near-black ground, raised tile tone, same colour tiles.

STORY: in one viewport the visitor sees who Dani is, that he is building BrowserOS now, his numbers, latest writing, talks and personality photos; they click into projects or posts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 6-column bento at 1440: hello tile with avatar, name and bio (2x2), speaking photo (2x2), blue BrowserOS tile with the primary "Explore the work" action (2x1), yellow stars tile, cosplay photo, latest-writing list (2x2), hike photo (1x2), ink globe tile (2x1), pink talks tile (1x2), Now and Uses link tiles (1x1 each). Socials live in the Connect tile below and in the footer.

Amended after the finish review: socials tile replaced by Now and Uses tiles (it duplicated Connect and the footer, and /now and /uses had no presence on the grid); green widened to cover open-source project tiles.

FORM: Bento Grid, position 1 of the safer-register lineup chosen by the user. seed:fde37a61e7fc0d35

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: tiles respond to hover with a small lift of colour (fill shift, never shadow); the globe tile spins slowly and links to /about.

Unresolved: none.
