# Surface brief: daniakash.com (home screen version)

Scope: all routes of daniakash.com; the homepage leads. Mode: Persuade on home; Read on posts, newsletter issues and resume; Experience-lite on projects, speaking and uses.

Audience: developers and AI builders. Action: explore the work, which means opening one of Dani's many AI tools, open-source libraries or apps. Proof: real app icons from each repo, real repo descriptions, npm packages, 29 talks, 16 posts, real photos. Dani's own steer: showcase lots of tools, libraries and apps, and show his taste. Constraints: every URL, feed and structured-data entity preserved; existing copy unchanged; new catalog entries come only from public repo names, descriptions and npm ownership verified on the registry.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the site is Dani's home screen: every AI tool, library and app he has made sits on it as a real icon you can open, with widgets for who he is and what he is building; refuses the long project list and the dark terminal portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: a calm wallpaper ground (pale lavender light, deep ink dark), real app icons as rounded squircles with names beneath, language or npm glyph icons for projects without one, flat white widgets with large radii and no borders, folders that open in place, a floating dock for the site sections; Archivo for display, Hanken Grotesk for text, one blue accent for live state; no glass, no gradients, no shadows beyond the dock's single soft lift.

STORY: in one viewport the visitor meets Dani in the hello widget, sees BrowserOS in the now widget, and scans a whole screen of his tools; tapping any icon opens its page or repo, and the dock takes them to writing, talks and about.

FIRST VIEWPORT: status bar with dani_akash_ and the theme toggle; a 8-column icon grid: hello widget (3x2) with avatar, name, role and bio; app icons for BrowserOS, aalai, Agent Terminal, herbie, Screen Time Overlay; speaking photo widget (2x2); stars and talks widgets; a now-building widget; AI Tools and Open Source folders; the dock pinned at the bottom with Blog, Newsletter, Speaking, About, Uses, Now.

FORM: Home Screen, Impeccable's pick in the third hand of a fresh roll, chosen by Dani. seed:4fa3043e7059bef3

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: folders open in place into a larger panel showing every icon and name, and icons press down like a real tap; inner pages open as app sheets with their app icon in the header and the dock still present.

Unresolved: whether Dani wants the earlier-work entries (OSlash, Pickyourtrail, book, course, docs) on the home screen or only in the projects app.
